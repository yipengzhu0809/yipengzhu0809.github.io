About

I am a second-year PhD student at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, working on visual perception for cameras and robots operating beyond conventional fields of view.

My research focuses on omnidirectional perception, 3D scene representation, visual localization, and underwater robotic systems. I received my bachelor's degree from Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen.
